About RR Nagar — Extended

RR Nagar was established in 1987 and is located off Mysore Road. The name Raja Rajeswarinagar was nominated by the founder with the blessing of Thiruchi Swamiji and was printed on the Possession Certificate in 1989. RR Nagar is the founder's brainchild, and he is the architect and developer of this layout, which consists of 6,000 sites of various dimensions. The total population of RR Nagar is 79,000.

Development & Planning

The development of RR Nagar has exceeded the founder's expectations and dreams. The sites were distributed for Rs.13 per sqft in 1988, Rs.18 per sqft in 1990, and Rs.25 per sqft in 1992.

The growth and value of this layout is comparable to Infosys shares. Assuming 1 Share Value = 1 Sqft land value, Infosys shares in 1988 were Rs.70 to Rs.80 per share, whereas 1 Sqft land in 1987 to 1992 given in RR Nagar by BEML Society was Rs.13 to Rs.25. The highest rate of Infosys share value was Rs.1480/- in 2020, including dividends and bonuses it would be around Rs.6500/-.

In 2020, BEML Society sites in RR Nagar had a value of Rs.12,000/- to Rs.15,000/- per sqft, and today BEML Society sites in RR Nagar are worth Rs.15,000 to Rs.20,000/- per sqft.

Community Initiatives

RR Nagar has five well-established engineering colleges and one medical college within a radius of 3KM. There are more than 75 schools, including international schools, CBSE, ICSE, and state syllabus schools. There are also 15 hospitals and nursing homes, more than 10 to 12 malls, more than 25 gyms, and all brand houses, clothes shops, and hotels. There are many apartments built by brand builders. RR Nagar is full of greenery with lots of plants and parks. It also has 40 feet, 60 feet, and 80 feet wide roads, beautiful clubs, and famous temples such as Rajarajeswari Temple, Shanmukha Temple, Eswara, Sangemeshwara, Byreshwara, Arunachaleswara, Raghavendra Sai baba, Sri Rama, Gayatri, Lord Venkateshwara, Kanyaka Parameshwari, Shaneshwara, Maruti, and Panchamukhi Ganesha Temples. This is called the temple hub and also education hub.

Nearby, there is Global Village IT Park, a metro railway station, and the NICE road, which connects to Hosur within 45 minutes and an Expressway connecting to Mysore within 75 to 90 minutes, making it a well-connected city.
